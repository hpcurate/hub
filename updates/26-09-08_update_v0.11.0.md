# v0.11.0 — card effects and refresh progress

**Project:** hub (hub extension)
**Date:** 2026-09-08

The card editor opens with recent-upload styling: glow, edge, tint or minimal;
sheen, breathe, ripple or no animation; accent, category or custom colour;
intensity, cycle length, name tint and an optional fresh-upload badge. The
default freshness window remains 24 hours. Normal, fresh and refreshing preview
states use the same renderer as the board. Fresh styling expires while HUB
stays open, and future upload timestamps no longer count as recent uploads.

Logo backgrounds have soft, monochrome, vivid, duotone, drifting and hover-zoom
effects, with opacity, position and blur controls. Cards also offer lift, zoom,
glow or no movement on hover and keyboard focus. Motion off and the operating
system's reduced-motion preference stop continuous effects.

The extension's toolbar now has a refresh button with a spinning icon, progress
bar and completion status. Cards animate while their requests are active.
Refreshing disables duplicate requests, and requests time out after 15 seconds.
Failed feed checks preserve known uploads and remain eligible for retry.

A lit card now has ten animations — none, sheen, breathe, ripple, pulse,
shimmer, scan, orbit, flicker and bounce — plus the curve they run on, the
direction they run in, and a stagger of up to 500ms between one card and the
next. Decorative effects can also be limited to cards that still carry an
unread dot. Cards arrive with fade, rise, drop, scale, slide, flip or blur, at a
chosen length and per-card delay; hover answers with nine effects and one
strength dial; the avatar zooms, spins, tilts or bounces; the new-video dot
pulses, blinks, pings or bounces; and a channel being checked sweeps, pulses,
bars, blinks, dims or does nothing.

Logo backgrounds gained sepia, glass, pan and rotate, fit (cover, contain,
tile), six fade masks, five blend modes, colour, contrast, angle and hover-boost
dials, opacity up to 100%, and seven overlays drawn in the category colour, the
accent, the page ground or a custom one. Under all of it is the card ground: a
category, accent or custom tint at a chosen strength, with a top, diagonal,
radial or edge gradient.

The latest-video box has its own section: only-on-fresh-uploads, hover, always
or click reveal, three thumbnail sizes, and switches for the thumbnail, the
title, the age and the actions. One channel can refuse the box on its own —
**latest video: on / off** in its edit pane, beside the pin — which travels with
the channel in an export.

Settings persist and travel with JSON exports. Older boards receive defaults.
The behavior harness covers the new controls, import validation, refresh
lifecycle, failures, timeouts and duplicate requests. `test/visual.cjs` checks
effects and mobile layout in Chromium and writes screenshots to ignored
artifacts; its screenshot settle is now framed and capped, because an offscreen
window only advances its animation timeline when a frame is asked for.
