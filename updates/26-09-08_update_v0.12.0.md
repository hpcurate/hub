# v0.12.0 — more animations, a hide button on the box, and a background for the page

**Project:** hub (hub extension)
**Date:** 2026-09-08

## Animations

Eight more ways for a fresh card to move, for eighteen in all: **glint** (one
bright pass and then a long wait), **wave** and **float** (the card itself,
which is what a stagger is for), **halo** (a ring leaving the card), **corners**
(a viewfinder round it), **rays**, **neon** (a tube striking) and **aurora** (a
slow band of colour behind everything). Alongside them: two more curves (snap
and elastic), a fourth direction (alternate-reverse), a **loop count** so a lit
card can settle down after three passes rather than never stopping, a stagger
that now runs to 1500ms, and **where the wave starts** — the first card, the
last, the middle, both edges at once, or nowhere in particular.

Hover has five more answers — shake, swing, flip, outline and saturate — and a
**speed** of its own; a card was moving on the same tenth of a second the sheet
uses for a button. Cards arrive six more ways (pop, swing, unfold, zoomout, wipe
and spin) on a curve of their own, with the stagger raised to 300ms. The avatar
gained flip, pop, shake, swing and glow; the new-video dot gained wobble,
breathe, flash and a ring that goes round it; a channel being checked can now
glow, stripe or spin.

Fixed: shimmer, scan, orbit and flicker each set `animation` as a shorthand
after the rule that had just set their delay, so the stagger did nothing on four
of the ten animations that had one.

## The latest video box

**A hide button on the box itself.** It writes the channel's own `hidePreview` —
the same switch the edit pane has carried since v0.11.0 — but from where you are
actually standing when you decide you never want that channel's uploads
announced. The button is a setting of its own, and the box now appears with a
fade, a slide, an expand or a zoom rather than a cut.

## Backgrounds

The wash effect list runs to eighteen: **invert**, **bloom**, **breathe**,
**sway**, **kenburns** (the slow documentary push) and **tilt** (three
dimensions under the pointer) join the twelve. Four new dials go with them —
**brightness**, **hue**, **how far the fade reaches** before the picture is
gone, and **how fast the moving ones move**, all six of them on one number.

Four more masks (right, vignette, corner, edges), three more fits (width,
height, stretch), and the blend list is now the browser's own sixteen rather
than five of them. Six more overlays — dots, noise, diagonal, mesh, frame and
corners — plus an **angle** and a **blend mode** for the overlay itself.

The card ground gained four gradients (bottom, conic, corner, sweep), a strength
dial for all of them, a tint that comes up under the pointer, a **shadow**
(soft, deep, glow, inner) and a **texture** — noise, grain, grid, dots, lines or
crosshatch, at its own opacity and its own size. A texture is the one background
that is neither a picture nor a colour, and it is what keeps a card a card once
there is something behind it.

Internally the wash's `filter` chain is now written once instead of in every
effect, which is why brightness and hue work in all eighteen rather than in the
one they were added to.

## The page behind the board

The largest background on the board was the one behind it, and it had no dial at
all. Now: **plain, gradient, glow, grid, dots, noise, vignette, aurora and
rays**, in the accent or a colour of its own, at a chosen strength, pattern
size and angle, with **let it move** off by default — a background that never
stops is the one thing a board left open all day cannot have. Every answer draws
over the ground rather than replacing it, so `plain` is exactly the board as it
was and none of them can leave the cards floating.

All of it saves, travels with a JSON export, and is part of a saved look; older
boards take the defaults. Motion off and the system's reduced-motion setting
still stop everything. The harness covers the hide button, the stagger orders,
the texture, the page background and the clamping of every new dial — 621
checks, all passing.
