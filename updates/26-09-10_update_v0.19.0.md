## What was done

These read as two tasks and are one bug seen from two sides.

### What was actually wrong

A card holding a video panel reserved `min-height:220px` **whether or not the
preview was showing**. In `hover` mode — the default — the preview is hidden
almost all the time, so every card with a new video was permanently tall for
content nobody was looking at. That is task 1: the card's size did not change,
because it had already changed and stayed that way.

And the preview arrived by switching `display:none` → `display:grid`. `display`
is the one property nothing can animate, so the card jumped. No speed setting
could reach it because there was no animation for a speed to apply to. That is
task 2, and why "nothing seems to affect that specific animation speed" was
literally true — `previewSpeed` was reaching the panel's *contents* fading in,
never the card getting bigger.

### The fix

**At rest the card is its own size.** The 220px reservation now belongs to
`always` mode, where the panel really is always there. In `hover` mode the card
is `--card-height` like every other card.

**On hover it doubles.** The panel is absolutely positioned at `top:100%` with
`height:100%` — 100% of the card is exactly one more card, which is the
"doubles it's height" that was asked for.

**The grid never moves.** This is the half that matters most and the reason the
panel is positioned rather than in flow: a grid item that grows makes its whole
row grow, every neighbour in that row stretches with it, and everything below
shifts down. That is the gap-and-shuffle the comment asked to stop. Positioned,
the card's own box never changes, so the grid is not re-laid at all — the grown
half is simply drawn over the row beneath, with `overflow:visible` and a raised
`z-index` on the hovered card only.

Un-clipping a card cannot leak anything: every layer inside one (`.wash`,
`.ground`, `.logo-overlay`, the `::after` tint) is an `inset:0` rectangle, so it
is bounded by the card anyway — and each now takes `border-radius:inherit`, so
their square corners cannot show at the card's rounded ones once the clip is
off.

**And it has a speed.** `previewGrowSpeed` — *card growth on hover* in settings →
animation, 0–2s, 0.26 by default — writes `--preview-grow`, and the panel
transitions `height`, `padding` and `opacity` against it. Its own dial rather
than the reveal's, because they are two different movements: the reveal is the
panel's contents arriving, this is the card getting bigger. `0` is instant, and
`prefers-reduced-motion` drops the transition entirely.
